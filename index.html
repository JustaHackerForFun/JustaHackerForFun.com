About Me

My CyberSecurity Journey

It started off as just interest, I was around 10 years old, I was always left in awe when hearing about stories of others hacking into companies and banks, and then I decided I also wanted to be able to hack into things(ethically ofcourse), and then at the age of 13 I took my interest and turned it into a passion. I started learning more and more about cybersecurity and slowly started shifting towards CTFs, Bug Bounties, and Penetration testing. At the age of 14 was the first time I found a session hijacking/fixation vulnerability and ever since then I've found countless vulnerabilities such as total account takeover, XSS, IDORs etc. Then at the age of 15 I decided to participate in a CyberSecurity BootCamp by NCSA and placed first in all of their competitions. I went on to participate in a CTF hosted by White Hat Desert and was able to qualify as a Finalist. If you wish to see my certification please contact me through Whatsapp/Email
